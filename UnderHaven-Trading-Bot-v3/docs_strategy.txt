UNDERHAVEN STRATEGY IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

Source architecture:
https://github.com/bored2boar/BORED-GROK-ARBITRAGE

Implemented architecture:
watcher -> executable order-book pricing -> fee engine -> safety margin -> paper telemetry -> live FOK execution -> optional merge -> persistent logs

Core invariant:
BOTH LEGS OR NEITHER.

Important limitation:
Two independent exchange orders are not an atomic transaction. The bot therefore does not claim guaranteed live profit. It uses executable depth, current fee estimates, FOK orders, equal share sizing, a daily loss stop, and immediate live-mode shutdown on an unconfirmed second leg.
